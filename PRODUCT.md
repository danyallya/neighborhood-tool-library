# Product brief

Problem: Reserve a tool, record handover condition and close a loan safely.

Audience: neighbors and community groups

## First release

One user role, one core record (loans) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Define ownership, deposit, damage and identity rules before exchanges.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
